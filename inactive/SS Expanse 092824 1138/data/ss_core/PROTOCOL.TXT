TIMESINCEDEATH
0: Used for things are activated as soon as the player dies.
1: Used ONLY for technical things that MUST be activated as soon as the player respawns.
2: Used for giving items, effects, etc. upon respawn. This is used for non-essential scores as well

SCORES
-00 scores, like the ones used for species 15, 16, and 17, are akin to ability scores.
They're decreased over time and used to determine if someone has access to a non-exclusive species/class.

POTION EFFECTS
Although this may be obvious, there are different times to use potion effects versus attribute modifiers.
Generally, use potion effects for non-stackable things, such as smaller bonuses and many strength/resistance bonuses, since those become overpowered when stacked.
Use attribute modifiers for stackable or difficult-to-achieve things, or things that you want to still be potion-able on top of.
